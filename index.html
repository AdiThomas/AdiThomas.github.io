About

I'm a Software Engineer at Magna International, one of the world's largest automotive suppliers. I build the internal tools people use to run production lines: full-stack apps in Java and React, drill-down dashboards for quality alerts, downtime, line availability and weld parameters, and pipelines that pull operational data out of Databricks.

I'm finishing a Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Engineering (Co-op) at the University of Guelph. Before Magna I worked IT at the City of Mississauga and ATS Corporation, so I've seen what happens when systems break on a plant floor, and I build to avoid it.

Away from work: Formula 1 (I care more about the strategy calls and the data than the podium), cars in general, and a self-hosted cloud running on a Raspberry Pi at home.

Self-hosted cloud · 2026

PiVault

My own replacement for iCloud Photos. It runs on a Raspberry Pi 5, and a custom iPhone app backs up the camera roll and lets me browse, upload and download from anywhere over Tailscale, with no ports open to the internet.

  • FastAPI backend with a separate worker for thumbnails, HEIC and video, deduplicated by SHA-256.
  • React Native (Expo) app: timeline, viewer, files and background backup.
  • Three SSDs pooled with mergerfs plus SnapRAID parity, nightly encrypted backups and a monthly automated restore test.
  • One SD-card image provisions the whole Pi with Ansible; Prometheus and Grafana watch it.
